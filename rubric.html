Judging rubric

How you'll be judged

Both rounds are scored out of 100 against five criteria. Use this page to plan your proposal and your pitch — it's exactly what the judges will be looking for.

Round 1 · Proposal Round 2 · Pitch

Draft Criteria and points may change before registration opens. The final rubric will be posted here and on @spip.hack (opens in a new tab).

Score bands

For each criterion, judges pick the band that best fits your work, then give a score within it — shown as a share of that criterion's points.

  • Excellent 90–100%
  • Strong 70–89%
  • Developing 40–69%
  • Beginning below 40%

Round 1 · Online Preliminary

Proposal rubric

Every team's written proposal is judged blindly by SPIP judges — they won't see team or school names. The top 10 scores go through to the final.

100 points across 5 criteria

  1. 01

    Problem & understanding

    Defines a real problem in cities, who it affects and why it matters.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    A specific, well-evidenced problem with a clear group of people affected and its causes explained.
    Strong70–89%
    A clear problem with some supporting evidence.
    Developing40–69%
    The problem is broad, or mostly assumed rather than shown.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    The problem is unclear or not connected to cities.
  2. 02

    Innovation & creativity

    How original the idea is, and how far it goes beyond what already exists.

    25 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    A genuinely fresh idea, or a clever new twist on an existing one, with a clear explanation of what makes it different.
    Strong70–89%
    A creative idea that improves noticeably on existing solutions.
    Developing40–69%
    A familiar idea with only small changes.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Copies an existing solution, or shows little original thinking.
  3. 03

    Impact on the City of Tomorrow

    How much it would make cities healthier, more sustainable or better places to live.

    25 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    Strong, believable impact on health, sustainability or liveability, with a sense of how many people benefit.
    Strong70–89%
    Clear benefits, though the scale or who benefits is less developed.
    Developing40–69%
    Some benefit, but it is small or loosely linked to the theme.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Little or no link to the theme question.
  4. 04

    Feasibility

    Whether it could realistically be built and used — technology, cost, people and time.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    A realistic plan that names the resources needed and the main risks, with ways to handle them.
    Strong70–89%
    Mostly realistic, with a few gaps in the plan.
    Developing40–69%
    Possible in principle, but key practical questions are missing.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Unrealistic, or no thought given to how it would work.
  5. 05

    Clarity of the proposal

    How clearly and logically the proposal is written and organised.

    10 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    Well structured, concise and easy to follow, in clear English.
    Strong70–89%
    Clear overall, with minor gaps in structure or detail.
    Developing40–69%
    Hard to follow in places, or missing sections.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Disorganised or very hard to understand.

Round 2 · On-site Final

Pitch rubric

The 10 finalist teams pitch live at SPIP to a panel of external judges, followed by questions from the panel. The final is scored fresh.

100 points across 5 criteria

  1. 01

    Innovation & creativity

    How original the idea is, and what makes it stand out.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    A fresh, memorable idea, and the team makes clear what sets it apart.
    Strong70–89%
    A creative idea with a clear point of difference.
    Developing40–69%
    A familiar idea with only small changes.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Little original thinking.
  2. 02

    Impact

    How convincingly the team shows the difference it would make to city life.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    Compelling evidence of real benefit to health, sustainability or liveability.
    Strong70–89%
    Clear benefits, but less evidence of scale.
    Developing40–69%
    Benefits are stated more than shown.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Impact is unclear.
  3. 03

    Feasibility & next steps

    Whether the idea could work, and a sensible plan for making it happen.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    Realistic, with clear next steps, resources and risks covered.
    Strong70–89%
    Mostly realistic, with some gaps.
    Developing40–69%
    Key practical questions are left open.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Unrealistic or no plan.
  4. 04

    Pitch delivery

    Storytelling, structure, visuals, timing, and how the whole team presents.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    Engaging and well paced, with strong visuals, finished on time, and every member contributes.
    Strong70–89%
    Clear and organised, with minor issues in pacing or visuals.
    Developing40–69%
    Hard to follow in places, or over time.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Unclear, unprepared or well over time.
  5. 05

    Q&A

    How well the team answers the judges' questions.

    20 pts

    Excellent90–100%
    Confident, accurate answers that show deep understanding of the idea.
    Strong70–89%
    Answers most questions well.
    Developing40–69%
    Some answers are vague or off topic.
    Beginningbelow 40%
    Struggles to answer questions.

Scoring notes

  • Each judge scores on their own. Your score is the average across the panel.
  • Round 1 only decides the 10 finalists — everyone starts the final from zero.
  • If two teams tie, the higher Impact score wins.
