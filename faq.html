The event

What is SPIP Hack?

SPIP Hack is the first student-led, cross-school innovation hackathon at Satit Prasarnmit International Programme (SPIP), Bangkok. Teams come up with a creative solution to a prompt, then pitch it to judges.

What is this year's theme?

City of Tomorrow (เมืองแห่งอนาคต). Every team answers one question: How can we use innovation to make cities healthier, more sustainable, and better places to live?

Do I need to know how to code?

SPIP Hack is an innovation pitching competition: you're judged on your idea and how well you present it, first as a written proposal and then, for finalists, as a live pitch. See the judging rubric for exactly what counts.

What language is the competition in?

English — for both the proposal and the pitch.

Teams & eligibility

How big can a team be?

Each team has 2–4 members, so you'll need at least one teammate.

Can my team include students from other schools?

Yes — teams can mix members from multiple schools. A multi-school team counts towards the quota of every school its members come from.

How many teams can one school enter?

Up to 3 teams per school. The competition is open to any school.

Registration & Round 1

How do I register?

Registration hasn't opened yet. Follow @spip.hack (opens in a new tab) on Instagram — we'll announce it there first.

What do we submit in Round 1?

A written proposal, submitted online in English. The exact format and deadline will be announced when registration opens.

How is Round 1 judged?

Proposals are judged blindly by SPIP judges — they don't see team or school names, so only the idea counts. The Round 1 rubric shows the criteria.

How many teams reach the final?

The top 10 teams from Round 1 go through to the on-site final.

The final

When and where is the final?

The Innovation Final is in March 2027 (exact date to be announced) at the 4th floor Mini Theatre, SPIP — 176 Sukhumvit 23, Wattana, Bangkok 10110. It's a short walk from BTS Asok and MRT Sukhumvit.

Who judges the final?

A panel of external judges. Teams pitch live and then answer the panel's questions — see the pitch rubric.

Do finalists need to attend in person?

Yes — the final is an on-site, live pitching round at SPIP.

Prizes & certificates

What can we win?

The Proposed prize pool is ฿10,000 for the top 3 teams: 1st ฿5,000, 2nd ฿3,000, 3rd ฿2,000.

Does everyone get a certificate?

Yes — all participants and winners receive certificates.
